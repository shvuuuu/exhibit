# Hackathon research (2026-10-20)

Goal: find the best hackathon, in Mumbai or online, with strong prizes and a good fit for a builder of AI agents (KaDeep AI: AI QA agents, browser automation, agentic products), where a complete product can be built now and submitted.

## Why PayPal

1. **Building now is allowed.** The rules accept new projects made during Oct 1 – Nov 12, so the product can be finished before the deadline.
2. **Many ways to place.** A project can win one grand prize (1st $12K, 2nd $8K, 3rd $5K) or an honorable mention (Most Creative, Most Impactful, Best Demo Delivery, Best Use of PayPal + AI, Best Use of Agentic Commerce: $5K each), **plus** one sponsor prize (AG Grid $5K / $2K / $1K×3, APIMatic, Bryntum, Channel3, Render credits).
3. **No prescribed problem statements**, so the problem is ours to pick. That favours a deep, real product over a generic chatbot.
4. **Judging** is five equal criteria: technological implementation, design, potential impact, innovation, presentation. Stage one is a pass/fail check that PayPal APIs are meaningfully used.

## Problem chosen

There are no official problem statements. The candidate problems were scored against the judging criteria:

| Idea | PayPal depth | Impact | Novelty | Demo-ability | Profile fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI dispute and chargeback agent** (Exhibit) | Disputes, Orders, Payments, Webhooks; full sandbox lifecycle | High: real money, deadlines, SMBs | Agentic investigation with cited evidence; honest refunds | Strong: webhook → trace → PDF → "Won" | Evidence-first agents = KaDeep's core |
| AI shopping assistant with PayPal checkout | Orders | Medium | Low (crowded) | Good | Medium |
| Invoice/collections agent | Invoicing | Medium | Low | Good | Medium |
| Checkout QA agent for PayPal integrations | Orders + negative testing | Medium (developers) | Medium | Harder | High |

Exhibit wins on impact, PayPal depth and story. Target prizes: a grand prize, or Most Impactful / Best Use of PayPal + AI, plus the **AG Grid** sponsor prize (the inbox and Protection tables are AG Grid).
